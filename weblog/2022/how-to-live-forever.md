# How to Live Forever

Notes from a snowy walk with my son.
* Have a family and spend time with them.
* Write about what you learn and share it with others.
* Treat others with kindness, or at the very least, charity.
* Help your community and give back where you can.
* Find something useful to work on.
* Try not to let any of the above interfere with any other. In case of conflict, prioritize family above all else.
* Repeat the above every day.
