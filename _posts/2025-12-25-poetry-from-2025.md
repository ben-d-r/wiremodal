## A Theory of Entanglement
*June 2025*

There are many things  
They don't tell you before you're a father  
The mess  
The stress  
The joy  
The pain  
The wonder  
The curiosity  
The little moments that slip away like sand through fingers.  
But above all:  
Your life is not yours  
You belong to another  
And they belong to you  
Forever together  
Impossible to separate  
A beautiful mess  
A mingling of souls  
Entangled, completely  

## Red Water
*August 2025*

The Great Lake  
Is completely calm  
Perfectly peaceful  
The water runs red  
With the light of the dying sun  
The shrieks of excited children fill the air  
So far removed from a world that  
Is completely chaotic  
Perfectly panicked  
The water runs red  
With the blood of dying innocents  
The shrieks of grieving mothers fill the air  
A world away and yet a part of  
The Great Lake
